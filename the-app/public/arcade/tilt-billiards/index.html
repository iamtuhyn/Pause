Tilt Billiards

No cue. Tip the whole table and let the balls roll home in order: 1, then 2, then 3. Pot the wrong one and it wanders back to the middle. Take your time.

Mouse: point where you want the balls to roll; the further from the centre, the steeper. Keyboard: hold the arrow keys. Touch: drag on the table, or use phone tilt. R restarts the table.
