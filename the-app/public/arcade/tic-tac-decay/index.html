The twist: each player can only have 3 marks on the board. When you place a 4th, your oldest mark disappears. The mark that's about to go fades and gets a dashed outline, and the dots under each mark show how long it has left. Get three in a row with the marks you have. Matches are first to 3 wins, and who goes first switches every game.
