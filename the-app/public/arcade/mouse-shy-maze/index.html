Mouse-Shy Maze

Hover over the glow to pick it up, then guide it to the green exit, no clicking. The walls shy away from your pointer and the doors slide open in their own time. Go slowly: there's no clock.

Mouse: just hover. Touch: drag with one finger, starting on the glow. Bumps are counted, not punished: slow and steady wins gold. This game needs a pointer; there is no keyboard version.
